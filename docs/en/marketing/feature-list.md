# CRM – Feature List

Author: Kristián Kačinetz · version 0.1.0

## Customers
- Companies with company ID, tax ID, VAT ID, address and contacts
- Contact persons with a primary contact flag
- Complete customer history: invoices, quotes, orders, unpaid amounts
- Quick search by name, company ID, e-mail

## Invoices
- Automatic status: Unpaid, Due soon, Overdue, Partly paid, Paid, Cancelled
- Filters and totals: how much is invoiced and how much is still to be paid
- Manual payments (full and partial), no bank connection needed
- Manual invoice entry

## iDoklad import
- Invoice list export (.xlsx, .csv; UTF-8 and Windows-1250)
- Automatic column detection, saved mapping
- Preview before import: new, changed, unchanged, failed rows
- Repeatable import without duplicates; manual payments are never overwritten
- Automatic customer matching by company ID or name

## Sales
- Price quotes with line items, units, discounts and VAT rates
- Professional PDF quotes with your logo and company details
- Copy quotes, custom numbering (e.g. CP2026001)
- Pipeline: board with stages Quote, Negotiation, Won, Lost (editable), drag and drop
- Orders from a won quote in one click, delivery statuses

## Overview and alerts
- Overview: overdue, total unpaid, open quotes, active orders
- Daily system alert for you only; nothing is sent to customers

## Data and security
- Everything stored locally, works offline
- No cloud, no telemetry, no monthly server fees
- Daily automatic backups to a folder of your choice (e.g. OneDrive)
- Ready to move to a shared database for more users later

## Platforms
- Windows 10 and 11 (primary), macOS and Linux

> The user interface is currently in Slovak.
