Desktop CRM for small businesses

Customers, invoices and deals in one place. On your own computer.

CRM is a simple application for the managing director or owner of a company. It shows who owes you money, which invoices are overdue, which quotes are awaiting a decision and what is being delivered. You keep issuing invoices in iDoklad; CRM reads them from the export.

What you get

Receivables at a glanceInvoice status is computed automatically: due soon, overdue, partly paid, paid.
iDoklad import.xlsx or .csv export, preview of changes, repeatable import without duplicates.
PDF price quotesLine items, discounts, VAT and a professional PDF with your logo.
Sales pipelineBoard with Quote, Negotiation, Won, Lost. Drag and drop.
OrdersFrom a won quote in one click, with delivery statuses.
Alerts for you onlyDaily due-date alert. Nothing is ever sent to customers.

Why local

  • Your data never leaves your computer. No telemetry.
  • Works offline, instantly.
  • Automatic daily backups, e.g. to OneDrive.
  • Ready to grow to multiple users.

Who it is for

Directors and owners of small companies and sole traders who invoice in iDoklad and want an overview of customers, receivables and deals without a complex, expensive CRM.
